# Expert Structural Similarity Study Ethics Checklist

This checklist prepares the study workflow. It does not determine whether the
university or another institution requires formal ethics approval.

Before collecting ratings, the project team must:

- Confirm with the university or responsible institution whether ethics
  approval, exemption, registration, or another review is required.
- Obtain informed consent in the form required by that institution.
- Explain that the task evaluates character structure similarity, not the
  participant's artistic skill or personal ability.
- Collect only the minimum participant metadata needed for the study.
- Use anonymized evaluator IDs in analysis files.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary names, phone numbers, student numbers, account
  identifiers, or other sensitive personal information.
- Explain data retention, withdrawal, and publication procedures.
- Confirm that reference-image licenses permit the planned local rating
  interface. Do not redistribute restricted source images through Git.
- Keep the evaluator-facing form blinded to OneStroke scores and pair types.
- Record any exclusions and missing ratings without silently dropping them.

The scripts in this repository do not generate or imitate human ratings.
Actual expert scores must come from consenting human evaluators.
