\title[Reference-conditioned calligraphy structure analysis]
{Reference-Conditioned Multi-Label Stroke Structure Analysis for
Low-Resource Chinese Calligraphy Practice}

\author*[1]{\fnm{Xiaofan} \sur{Liu}}\email{[AUTHOR EMAIL]}
\author[1]{\fnm{Ronghao} \sur{Zhang}}

\abstract{%
\textbf{Purpose}\quad Digital calligraphy systems should expose local
structure, compare practice against an intended reference, and provide
diagnostic evidence without presenting an opaque score as expert judgment. We
study this problem in a low-resource Chinese calligraphy setting.

\textbf{Methods}\quad Each character is represented by five overlapping
direction channels and one keypoint channel. U-Net, DeepLabV3+, and
SegFormer-B2 are evaluated with three seeds on QC-clean standard and
character-disjoint splits. Constrained alignment and fixed structure agreement
are further assessed on 200 cached references using perturbation, ablation,
score-audit, diagnostic, and blinded human-validation experiments.

\textbf{Results}\quad DeepLabV3+ achieves standard-split Macro Dice
\(0.9630\pm0.0006\); SegFormer-B2 leads character-disjoint evaluation at
\(0.8866\pm0.0008\). Relative to no alignment, the constrained method reduces
nuisance penalties by 30.118 points (95\% confidence interval
29.506--30.727) while preserving 11.189 additional points of structural
penalty (10.094--12.322). Forty-one references contain inactive direction
channels, with coverage corrections up to 15.402 points. On 150 natural
same-character pairs, the production score correlates with mean blinded human
ratings at \(\rho=0.297\) (0.148--0.441), versus \(\rho=0.429\)
(0.313--0.535) for the coverage-aware audit; average-rating reliability is
ICC(2,\(k\)) \(=0.709\).

\textbf{Conclusion}\quad The pipeline provides auditable, human-validated
structural evidence but is not an expert-calibrated measure of calligraphic
beauty. Writer-disjoint robustness remains unresolved because reliable writer
identities are unavailable.}

\keywords{Chinese calligraphy, stroke segmentation, multi-label parsing,
reference-based assessment, image registration, diagnostic feedback}
