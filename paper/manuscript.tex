\title[Reference-conditioned calligraphy structure analysis]
{Reference-Conditioned Multi-Label Stroke Structure Analysis for
Low-Resource Chinese Calligraphy Practice}

\author[1]{\fnm{Ronghao} \sur{Zhang}}

\abstract{%
\textbf{Purpose}\quad Digital calligraphy-learning systems require more than
whole-character recognition: they must expose local structural evidence,
compare a practice sample against an intended reference, and express
diagnostic suggestions without presenting an opaque aesthetic score as expert
judgment. We study reference-conditioned structural analysis in a
low-resource Chinese calligraphy setting.

\textbf{Methods}\quad We represent each character with five overlapping
direction-oriented stroke-region channels and one keypoint channel. A
segmentation model predicts this representation, constrained similarity
alignment suppresses limited placement variation, and a fixed agreement
function combines direction overlap, foreground overlap, and tolerant
keypoint agreement. Post-segmentation behaviour is evaluated on 200 real
cached references using controlled mask perturbations, paired alignment
ablation, a structure-score audit, cross-reference comparisons, and a
diagnostic benchmark.

\textbf{Results}\quad Translation is fully recovered in 800 valid trials,
whereas rotation and especially scale remain challenging. Relative to no
alignment, the constrained method reduces nuisance penalties by 30.118 points
(95\% confidence interval 29.506--30.727) while preserving an additional
11.189 points of structural penalty (10.094--12.322). Forty-one of 200
references contain inactive direction channels, producing coverage
corrections up to 15.402 points. On 150 natural same-character pairs, the
production score correlates with the mean blinded human structural-similarity
rating at \(\rho=0.297\) (95\% CI 0.148--0.441), versus
\(\rho=0.429\) (0.313--0.535) for the coverage-aware audit. Average-rating
reliability is ICC(2,\(k\)) \(=0.709\).

\textbf{Conclusion}\quad The pipeline provides auditable structural evidence
and a human-validated structural signal, while exposing clear failure modes.
It is not an expert-calibrated measure of calligraphic beauty. Main
segmentation, character-disjoint, and smartphone results remain explicit
pending experiments until real evidence is available.}

\keywords{Chinese calligraphy, stroke segmentation, multi-label parsing,
reference-based assessment, image registration, diagnostic feedback}
