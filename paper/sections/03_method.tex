\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Overlapping six-channel representation}

For an RGB character image \(I\), the parser predicts
\[
P=f_{\theta}(I)\in[0,1]^{H\times W\times 6}.
\]
The channel order is fixed as
\[
(\mathrm{vec1},\mathrm{vec2},\mathrm{vec3},\mathrm{vec4},
\mathrm{vec5},\mathrm{keypoint}).
\]
The first five masks represent direction-oriented stroke regions; the sixth
represents keypoint evidence. Because crossing or adhering strokes may support
multiple direction labels at the same pixel, each channel uses an independent
sigmoid. A softmax would incorrectly force mutual exclusion.

Images are converted to RGB and letterboxed to \(512\times512\), preserving
aspect ratio. Masks use nearest-neighbour resizing. Training losses combine
class-weighted binary cross entropy and Dice loss on the direction channels,
focal and Dice losses on the keypoint channel, and a boundary term. The current
frozen production parser is SegFormer-B2; architecture comparisons belong to
Task~1.

\subsection{Reference cache}

Each course item identifies a target character, style, and approved reference.
The frozen parser converts each reference image once into a binary
\(512\times512\times6\) cache. The cache is versioned by model, checkpoint
SHA-256, threshold set, channel schema, and reference identifier. It reduces
runtime cost but remains model-derived evidence; it is never used as
segmentation ground truth.

\subsection{Constrained reference alignment}

Let \(U\) and \(R\) be the user and reference binary masks. The production
alignment searches
\[
s\in[0.8,1.2],\qquad \phi\in[-3^\circ,3^\circ],
\]
with isotropic scale, small rotation, and centroid-derived translation. The
search maximizes direction-ink intersection-over-union. Nonuniform scale,
affine shear, and deformable warping are disabled. This deliberately restricts
the model's ability to ``repair'' a local mistake through registration.

\subsection{Structure agreement}

For the five direction channels, let \(D_c\) be the Dice agreement after
alignment and let
\[
\overline{D}=\frac{1}{5}\sum_{c=1}^{5}D_c.
\]
Let \(J_{\mathrm{ink}}\) be the IoU between the unions of the five direction
channels, and let \(F_{\mathrm{kp}}^{(3)}\) be keypoint F1 with a three-pixel
tolerance. The frozen production score is
\[
S=100\left(
0.55\overline{D}
+0.25J_{\mathrm{ink}}
+0.20F_{\mathrm{kp}}^{(3)}
\right).
\]
The weights are not retuned on the controlled benchmark. \(S\) is interpreted
as prototype structure agreement, not a calibrated calligraphy grade.

\subsection{Evidence extraction and diagnostics}

The system retains the score components, alignment transform, pre-alignment
center offset, foreground-area ratio, channel-level agreements, and keypoint
agreement. Direction-mask differences are summarized into a \(3\times3\)
spatial grid, producing a normalized finding with:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a finding category such as center offset, scale, keypoint relation, or
  local direction structure;
  \item an optional direction channel;
  \item missing-reference or extra-user structure;
  \item an optional grid region.
\end{itemize}
The deterministic evidence can be returned directly or rendered into text by a
separate language model. The latter is not required for segmentation, scoring,
or the diagnostic benchmark.
