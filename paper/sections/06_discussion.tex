\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Computer-vision and assessment findings}

The model comparison shows why low-resource dense parsing should be evaluated
on the intended generalization axis. DeepLabV3+ is strongest on the
QC-standard split, SegFormer-B2 leads character-disjoint direction overlap,
and U-Net is strongest for strict endpoints. The standard-to-disjoint gap
also shows that near-saturated in-domain overlap does not imply transfer to
unseen character structures. Independent sigmoid channels address a separate
representation problem: at adhered or crossing regions they preserve
multiple valid directional memberships that a foreground union or
mutually-exclusive partition cannot express.

Registration is part of the measurement design, not neutral preprocessing.
The bounded search substantially reduces nuisance penalties relative to no
alignment while retaining local structural penalties; a wider search does
not improve this trade-off. The claim is relative rather than invariant:
rotation and scale retain residual penalties, and scale-up and compound
summaries are conditional on clipping-safe references. The inactive-channel
audit likewise shows that macro overlap can reward absent structure.
Label-coverage semantics should therefore be audited whenever the active
multi-label set varies across samples.

The human study bounds the assessment claim. The frozen production score has
a modest positive association with mean ratings, the coverage-aware audit is
stronger, and the retrospectively developed ASDS is strongest. Because ASDS
features and weights were finalized after the same 150 ratings were
available, \(\rho=0.556\) is a development estimate, not external
confirmation. Character-grouped out-of-fold selection reduces
weight-selection optimism but cannot make the representation prospective;
the frozen specification should next be tested without modification on new
writers, characters, and raters.

The direct-ink control clarifies the role of parsing. ASDS uses only the union
silhouette, and parsed-union and direct-ink variants are nearly equivalent on
clean digital input. Parsing is therefore not justified as a way to improve
this scalar. Its independently evaluated value is channel-wise agreement,
simultaneous labels at crossings, endpoint relations, and localized
missing/extra directional evidence. A direction-label swap may preserve the
silhouette and ASDS while changing all of these outputs. Systems needing only
a scalar silhouette comparison may use direct ink; diagnostic systems still
require the parser.

Human and diagnostic uncertainty remain material. Aggregated ratings are
more reliable than individual ratings. The three author-raters had relevant
calligraphy qualifications---two held Level-9 certificates after more than
five years of formal study, and one was a fine-arts master's student
specializing in calligraphy---but a three-person author panel is not an
independent senior-expert cohort. The supported term is human-audited
structural agreement, not universal expert aesthetic grading. Exact-region
failures mainly reflect a single-cell metric applied to multi-cell
interventions, followed by direction-attribution errors. Future evaluation
should use region-set or pixel-overlap localization. Language generation must
remain downstream of structured evidence because fluent prose cannot repair
an incorrect direction, endpoint relation, or location.

\subsection{Limitations}

The segmentation corpus contains 40 labelled character identities from one
recovered acquisition source. The commissioned protocol involved
calligraphy-trained contributors, but trustworthy writer identities and
standardized credentials are unavailable; character-disjoint evaluation is
not writer-disjoint evaluation. Semantic QC removes known mismatches and
exact-duplicate leakage, yet collection-specific regularities may remain.

Controlled perturbations operate on cached reference masks to isolate
registration and score behaviour, whereas an end-to-end failure may combine
parsing, registration, and comparison errors. The external library contains
only seven same-character cross-style pairs and no same-style
different-instance pair. The human cohort contains 150 pairs rated by three
qualified but non-independent author-raters, and ASDS development is
retrospective. Finally, the target modality is clean direct digital writing;
paper texture, illumination, perspective, phone capture, historical
authenticity, and universal aesthetic quality are outside the present claims.
